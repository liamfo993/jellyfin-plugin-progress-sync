Pushover notifications

Send selected Jellyfin library, watch, and playback activity to a Pushover user or group.

Connect your Pushover account Create an application token in Pushover, then enter it with your user or group key. Credentials are stored on this server and are never returned to the browser. Leave credential fields blank to keep saved values.
